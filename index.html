Heads-Up Computing: AI-Mediated Interaction in Mixed Reality

Workshop Call for Papers · Date & venue TBA · Full CfP below

Wearable mixed reality's central design challenge has shifted from what to display to AI-mediated delivery: how should an agent decide what to surface, in what form, and when, under divided attention? As mass-market smartglasses and headsets emerge, real-time, context-sensitive mediation is feasible, yet shared design and evaluation frameworks are missing.

This workshop addresses AI-mediated MR interaction, where an AI agent retrieves, transforms, and times information — treating human attention as a finite resource to steward, grounded in resource-rational HCI. This marks a shift to Heads-Up Computing: the system adapts to the user’s state rather than the user adapting to the device.

Topics of Interest

We invite contributions organized around three interdependent challenges:

1. Sensing & Modelling Real-time attention and cognitive load estimation from commodity wearables; context and activity recognition; multimodal user state inference; computational models of user intention and behaviour for wearable MR.
2. Transformation & Delivery AI-driven information transformation and access for wearable media (text, video, audio); adaptive interruption and notification management; multimodal output selection (voice, gaze, peripheral display, haptics); delivery optimisation under divided attention.
3. Trust, Agency & Ethics User trust and control when AI suppresses or modifies information; social acceptability in public settings; privacy and surveillance implications; proactive vs. reactive AI behaviour; evaluation methodologies for real-world deployment.
